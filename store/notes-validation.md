APP PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Éclats Éternels is a free, offline incremental ("clicker") game. The player taps a central crystal to earn shards, buys upgrades that produce shards automatically, collects achievements and cosmetic styles, and can "rebirth" to restart with permanent bonuses. It offers short, relaxing play sessions for casual players of all ages (age rating 4+). It contains no violence, no ads and no in-app purchases.

HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login, sign-up or sample file is needed. Open the app and follow the yellow tutorial bubbles: tap the crystal, buy an upgrade in the "Améliorer" tab, then open the "Boutique" (cosmetic styles, backdrops, boosts) and "Succès" (achievements) tabs. The gear icon (top right) opens settings: sounds and vibrations, save export/import, rebirth, and the privacy policy link. Special events appear on their own: a golden crystal, item showers, a x10 production boost at shard milestones, and a daily reward on the first launch of each day. The 7-day streak and rebirth (which needs 10 million shards in total) are meant for longer play and are not required to review the app.

SCREEN RECORDING

The app has no account registration, login, account deletion, user-generated content or paid content, so none of these appear in the recording.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The game runs entirely on the device and works in airplane mode. It uses no analytics, ads, authentication, payment, data providers or AI services. The only external link is the privacy policy page, which opens in Safari.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works identically in all regions. Seasonal visuals (Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day, Easter) depend only on the device date.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. All graphics and text were created for this game. It uses no third-party protected material and does not operate in a regulated industry.
